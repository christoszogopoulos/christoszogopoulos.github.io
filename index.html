Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, Athens University of Economics and Business

Public finance · Political economy · Economic history

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of International and European Economic Studies at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB), supervised by Associate Professor Pantelis Kammas. My dissertation, Determinants and Economic Consequences of State Fiscal Capacity, combines historical panel data with modern causal-inference methods to study how states build the capacity to tax and spend, and what that capacity means for economic and political outcomes over the long run.

Department of International and European Economic Studies, Athens University of Economics and Business

Courses

Microeconomics II — Teaching Assistant, 2024–present

Main professor: Associate Professor Vasia Panousi

Course description: An intermediate microeconomics course building on Microeconomics I, with emphasis on mathematical rigor. It covers consumer theory under certainty, the theory of the firm and production, and market structures from perfect competition to monopoly, including the welfare effects of taxes, subsidies, and price controls. It also introduces consumer choice under uncertainty and behavioral economics.

Microeconomics I — Teaching Assistant, 2023–2024

Main professor: Professor Chrysovalantou (Valanta) Milliou

Course description: An introductory course in microeconomic theory, covering its fundamental concepts and tools and their application to real-world economic issues. Topics include supply and demand, consumer choice, production costs and profit, perfectly competitive and imperfectly competitive markets, market failures, and factor markets.

Macroeconomics I — Teaching Assistant, 2023–2024

Main professors: Professor George Economides and Assistant Professor Panagiotis Konstantinou

Course description: An introductory course in macroeconomic theory, covering the measurement of key aggregates such as GDP, consumption, investment, inflation, and unemployment, and their main determinants. It equips students with core tools for interpreting macroeconomic developments in Greece and globally, and for assessing the effects of fiscal and monetary policy.

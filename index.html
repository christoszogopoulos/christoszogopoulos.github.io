Christos I. Zogopoulos

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, Athens University of Economics and Business

Public finance · Political economy · Economic history

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of International and European Economic Studies at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB), working under the supervision of Associate Professor Pantelis Kammas. My dissertation, Determinants and Economic Consequences of State Fiscal Capacity, studies how states build the capacity to tax and spend, and what that capacity means for economic and political outcomes over the long run.

My current research combines historical panel data and modern causal-inference methods to understand the long-run relationship between war, state-building, and fiscal capacity.
